About the game

Short description

TicToe Fury is tic-tac-toe taken to a new level, for iPhone and iPad. Play the AI in easy or hard mode, challenge a friend on the same device, or play online through Game Center, with hand-drawn chalkboard graphics, more than 30 achievements and an original soundtrack.

Long description

TicToe Fury turns the game everyone grew up with into something you can keep playing for hours. Play alone against the AI in easy or hard mode, pass the device to a friend, or play online against a random player or one of your friends with Game Center turn-based multiplayer.

Every illustration is drawn by hand, and the images and animations are made separately for each device, so the game looks sharp on both iPhone and iPad. Players pick their name, colors and playing style, earn more than 30 achievements and climb the online leaderboard. The game is universal, and online matches sync across devices.

Version 4.0 rebuilt the online experience, added new graphics and animations, and brought support for every iOS device.

History

TicToe Fury was wOzy's first game, built when the first iPad came out. It started with pencil and paper: every graphic was sketched and drawn by hand, and the code was written from scratch. The team still updates it today; version 4.0 is a full rebuild of the online experience.
