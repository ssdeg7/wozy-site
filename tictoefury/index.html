Multiple game styles

TicToe Fury is built to become one of your must-have apps, and to keep you playing for hours. Play alone, with friends on your device or online. However you play, it'll keep you entertained.

Online or offline

Play against the AI in easy or hard mode, challenge a friend on the same device, or play online against a random player or one of your friends.

Amazing graphics

The game from your childhood, taken to a new level with artwork made for each device. Whether you play on iPhone or iPad, it looks great.

Every illustration is drawn with care, and images and animations are made separately for each device, so the graphics always look sharp.

Make it yours

Pick your name, your colors and your playing style, so every match feels different. Earn more than 30 achievements and climb the online leaderboard.

The game is universal: download it once and play on all your iOS devices. Your online matches sync too, so you can pick up where you left off.
