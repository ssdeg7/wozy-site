How 9x9 tic-tac-toe works

  1. The board is a big 3x3 grid made of nine small tic-tac-toe boards.
  2. The square you pick inside a small board sends your opponent to the matching board: play the top-right square, and they have to play in the top-right board.
  3. Get three in a row on a small board to win it.
  4. Win three small boards in a row to win the game.

Why is TicToe Fury better than regular tic-tac-toe?

Regular tic-tac-toe is solved: once both players know where to start and how to block, every game is a draw, and most players figure that out as kids. TicToe Fury keeps the rules everyone knows and adds one twist that changes everything. Because your move picks the board your opponent plays next, you're always playing two games at once: the small board in front of you and the big board that decides the match. You have to plan ahead, set traps, and sometimes give up a small board to win the big one. That's why a game of TicToe Fury feels like chess on a napkin, and why people keep playing it after more than a decade.
