THANKSGIVING UPDATE

Meet Turkey, the Racer Who Only Comes Around in November

Animal Loop's first seasonal racer is strutting onto the hills. Race through November and it joins your crew.

By Sebastian Salazar de Greiff · October 23, 2026 · 1 min read

For Thanksgiving, Animal Loop gets something new: a racer you can only earn at one time of year. Meet Turkey, a fan-tailed show-off with a banded tail fan, a red snood and a lot of confidence.

Other racers join you by beating a world's Grand Prix. Turkey is different. It doesn't care how fast you are. It wants to see you show up.

NOVEMBER
15
DAYS
HOW TO UNLOCK

Race on 15 days in November.

Any race counts, one per day, on any track. And if you don't make it this year, your days carry over: they add up every November until Turkey is yours.

It introduces itself

When November starts, Turkey turns up on the title screen. It peeks over the hills, hops up beside your racer, and sizes you up:

  1. Someone's strutting around the hills.
  2. TURKEY“I'm Turkey. I only come around in November.”
  3. TURKEY“I don't race with just anyone. Prove you're worth it this month, and I'll join your crew.”
  4. TURKEY“Think you can keep up?”

A title screen for every season

The update also dresses the title screen for the time of year: autumn leaves through November, then snow for winter, petals in spring and seeds drifting up in summer. Down south, the seasons flip, so the title screen follows the weather where you are.

Turkey is the first seasonal racer, but not the last. Happy Thanksgiving, and see you on the hills.
