SHORT DESCRIPTION
Animal Loop is a one-tap racing game for iPhone and iPad. Your animal rolls on
its own, and every tap turns it. Race 18 animals across 27 handmade tracks in
three worlds, take on a Grand Prix in every world, and race into the night as
the sun sets on every track.

LONG DESCRIPTION
In Animal Loop there is no stick and no brake. Your animal rolls forward in a
straight line inside its ball, and each tap turns it. Time your taps to stay
off the walls and keep your hearts.

The game spans three worlds, each with nine handmade tracks, from simple loops
to knots that cross over and under themselves. Dune Flats adds switch gates
that flip which way a tap turns you. Coral Shallows adds bridges and loops that
pass back under themselves. Fern Hollow adds sticky mud and moles that dig you
a shortcut.

Every world ends with a Grand Prix: all nine tracks back to back, at the speed
you pick, against that world's rival animal. Win it and the rival joins your
roster. Game Center friends race alongside you as ghosts of their best runs.
Every track also has its own day/night cycle, with shadows that follow the sun.

KEY FACTS
- 1 button: every tap turns your racer
- 3 worlds: Dune Flats, Coral Shallows and Fern Hollow
- 27 handmade tracks, 9 per world
- 18 collectible racers, from a hamster to an octopus
- A Grand Prix in every world, raced against a rival animal
- Game Center friends race as ghosts of their best runs
- A real-time day/night cycle on every track
- Track mechanics: bridges, switch gates, mud, mole tunnels and walls that crack

HISTORY
Animal Loop began around 2010 as "Hamster Racing", an idea sketched right after
wOzy launched its first game, TicToe Fury, for the first iPad. It was shelved.
Development restarted from a blank Xcode project on July 28, 2026. To balance
27 tracks for a game with a single input, Sebastián built a custom level
editor, a track analyzer that scores every track's difficulty, and a simulator
that plays every track with a bot.
