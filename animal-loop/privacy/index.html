Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Animal Loop ("the app") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. The app is made by wOzy Technologies LLC ("we", "us").

The short version

  • We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We don't show ads and we don't sell your data.
  • We collect anonymous gameplay analytics to understand how the game is played and make it better.

Information we collect

Anonymous player ID. When you first open the app, it creates a random identifier. It isn't connected to your name, email, or Apple Account. It's stored on your device and in your iCloud account, so your progress follows you if you reinstall the app or play on another device.

Gameplay and usage data. Through the analytics service Mixpanel, we record how the app is used, for example:

  • races started and finished, tracks played, speed settings, results, and times
  • which characters you select or unlock
  • which screens you visit, and progress through the intro story
  • achievement progress, star counts, and in-app settings
  • whether you're signed in to Game Center (yes or no only; we don't receive your Game Center profile)
  • app icon changes

Purchase information. When you make an in-app purchase, we record which item it was, its price and currency, and whether the purchase succeeded, failed, or was cancelled. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account information.

Services provided by Apple

  • iCloud stores your game progress and anonymous player ID in your own iCloud account so they sync across your devices. That data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Game Center handles leaderboards and achievements if you choose to sign in. Your Game Center profile and scores are managed by Apple under its privacy policy.
  • In-App Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Contacting support

If you email us from the app, the email includes the app version, iOS version, and your anonymous player ID, so we can help with your issue. We also receive your email address and anything you write. We use this only to respond to you.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • understand how players use the game and improve levels, difficulty, and features
  • diagnose problems
  • understand purchase trends
  • respond to support requests

We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Data retention

Analytics data is kept for as long as it's useful for improving the app, up to 24 months, and then deleted. Support emails are kept as long as needed to resolve your request.

Your choices and rights

  • To have your analytics data deleted, email us at info[at]wozy.co and include your player ID. To find it, start a support email from the app's Settings; the ID is included automatically.
  • You can stop all future collection by deleting the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Contact us to make a request.

Children

Animal Loop is a general-audience game. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The app doesn't ask for names, emails, or other contact details. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example through a support email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
